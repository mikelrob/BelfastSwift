About Belfast Swift

Belfast Swift is a community for iOS and Swift developers in Belfast. We get together to share knowledge, explore new ideas, and connect with fellow engineers passionate about building great apps for Apple platforms.

Whether you're a seasoned iOS pro or just starting out with Swift, you're welcome here. Come for the talks, stay for the conversations.
